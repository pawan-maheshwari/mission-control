Core pillars for first-attempt selection

Demonstrate heavy maths alignment

Admission committees at top French public institutions screen primarily for theoretical and applied mathematics prerequisites — multivariable calculus, stochastic processes and linear algebra. The transcript is read before the essay.

Submit inside the first fortnight

Get the dossier in within the first 14 days of the portal opening in October or November 2027, before international cohort quotas fill. Late-but-complete loses to early-and-complete.

Write a research-focused SOP

Avoid generic essays. Name specific tracks, elective units, thesis aspirations and the career alignment behind them — quantitative research, risk modelling or data science.

Run a referee protocol

University platforms email validation requests directly to referees. Brief both referees in advance, warn them the mail is coming, and follow up — an unvalidated letter silently sinks an otherwise complete dossier.

Before this drives any decision

One thing to confirm about the level. This roadmap is written for postgraduate entry — it references CGPA, MonMaster, M1 and M2 tracks, ECTS syllabus mapping and academic letters from university professors. If Fall 2028 is meant to follow Class 12 rather than a bachelor's degree, the route is a different one: undergraduate licence admission runs through Parcoursup or the Campus France DAP procedure, not MonMaster, and the prerequisite screen is the Class 12 mathematics record rather than a university transcript. The phases, the October discipline, the language certification and the visa steps all still hold — only the portals and the evidence change. Worth settling before October 2027, because the two routes open on different calendars.
