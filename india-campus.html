    0/11

      Before paying any deposit

      Offers land before ISI and IIMB results

      UNSW wants acceptance and a deposit within 21 days and allows no deferral. Liverpool takes 20% of the fee at acceptance, Southampton 10%. Pay only refundable amounts until the IIMB interview (Jan 2028) and ISI result (mid-2028) are known.

      Refunds follow the UGC 2024 notice

      York and Bristol publish the same ladder: full refund (minus up to ₹1,000) if you withdraw 15+ days before the last admission date, then 90%, 80%, 50%, then nothing after 30 days past it.

      Class XI marks count — now

      York Mumbai selects undergraduates on Class X and XI marks plus an interview. Liverpool accepts Class XI or pre-board scores for a conditional offer. Bhavin's Class 11 finals in early 2027 are part of the application.

      Predicted marks need paperwork

      UNSW accepts CISCE predicted grades only on school letterhead, signed, dated after 1 November of the year before entry, and two points above the published bar.

      English waiver via ISC

      Most campuses waive IELTS for 70–80% in Class XII English (Liverpool 70%, Bristol 70% for Data Science, York 75%, Aberdeen 70–80%). Below that, an IELTS 6.5 is the fallback.

      The degree is the home degree

      Graduates receive the same award as the UK or Australian campus. Most campuses also offer a semester at the home campus (Southampton in Year 2; York UK or Greece).
