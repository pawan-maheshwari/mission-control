Training Games

Speed is a trained muscle. Math Sprint builds the mental-arithmetic pace QADI demands; Formula Match hardwires recall under time pressure.

Game 01

⚡ Math Sprint — 60 seconds

Rapid arithmetic: percentages, fractions, squares, products. Each correct answer = +10, streak bonus every 5. Target: 150+ points.

Game 02

🧩 Formula Match

Match 8 concepts to their formulas against the clock. Under 45 seconds = elite recall.

Game 03

🎯 The Estimator — DI reflexes

The 2026 paper's DI was calculation-heavy. Train approximation: see a fraction, snap-judge the closest percentage. 10 rounds, 8 seconds each.

Game 04

🔢 Sequence Hunter — LR patterns

Number series under the gun: 8 sequences, 15 seconds each. Pattern-spotting is the whole LR section in miniature.

Game 05

📚 Vocab Blitz — EC ammo

12 words, 6 seconds each: pick the synonym before the timer eats the points. Builds the vocabulary reflex VA questions reward.

Mock Interview Studio

The PI is 40% of your composite score — it can rescue a borderline test score or sink a great one. Panels probe motivation, math fundamentals, data-science curiosity, academics, and current affairs. Run a 6-question session: speak your answer aloud (2 minutes each), then self-rate 1–5 against the rubric.

Projection Model & Assumptions

Drivers pushing cutoffs UP each year: (1) awareness — year 1 had a short 5-month runway from announcement to test; coaching institutes now run dedicated batches; (2) JEE/IPMAT crossover aspirants with heavy quant training; (3) fixed 40 DS seats even as applicant pool grows toward IIMB's stated scale-up. Reading The Composite Score

Final rank = 0.6 × (test score, normalized) + 0.4 × (PI score). At 40 seats, a 10-percentile PI swing ≈ several ranks. The winning profile: 130+ raw score AND a rehearsed, authentic interview. Neither alone is enough. Academic consistency in Class X/XII marks is reviewed at PI stage — protect your board scores; the 60%-in-Maths eligibility floor is a hard gate.
