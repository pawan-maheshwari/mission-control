The Score Band — where you must land

0 — Danger zone 114 · 2026 cutoff 135–180 · Safe zone

Strategy under the new pattern: 45 accurate attempts (of 60) at ≥90% accuracy ≈ 130–135 raw. Accuracy still beats volume — a wrong answer costs 1 mark and forfeits 3. Two changes to how you allocate effort: (1) you must score positive in all three sections, so no section can be written off; (2) inside the 70-point pre-PI test score, LR carries 30 points against QADI's 20 and VARC's 20 — with only 15 LR questions, each LR question is worth roughly three times as much pre-PI as each QADI question. LR is no longer the easy section you clear quickly; it is the highest-leverage section in the paper.

LR has half as many questions as QADI but 50% more pre-PI weight, so one LR question is worth roughly three QADI questions at the shortlisting stage. This is the single most actionable fact in the 2027 documents, and almost nobody's study plan reflects it. It does not mean you can neglect QADI: the 180-mark aggregate cut-off weights every question equally, the published percentile bar sits on QADI alone, and you must post a positive raw score in all three sections. The correct response is to stop treating LR as a section you clear in twenty minutes and start giving it daily time and its own error log.

Gates you must clear, in order
  • ▸ Gate 1 — Positive raw score in every section. Negative marking means an over-guessed section can go below zero and end your candidacy regardless of total.
  • ▸ Gate 2 — QADI percentile. 80th for General; 75th for NC-OBC and EWS; 70th for SC, ST and PwD.
  • ▸ Gate 3 — Aggregate cut-off. 114/180 for General in the published 2025 table.
  • ▸ Gate 4 — Pre-PI rank within your category, on the 100-point scale above.
  • ▸ Gate 5 — Post-PI score. Offers are made strictly on this, per category.

IIMB adds a sentence worth reading twice: the scores of those actually called for interview were higher than these cut-offs. 114 is a floor, not a target. Observers put the real General bar near the 98th percentile. Plan for 135–140.

Three corrections to what this app previously said. (1) The published QADI bar is the 80th percentile for General, not the 85th. (2) "NA" on VARC and LR does not mean they are ignored — a positive raw score in every section is a separate hard gate. Projection Model & Assumptions

Drivers pushing cutoffs UP each year: (1) awareness — year 1 had a short 5-month runway from announcement to test; coaching institutes now run dedicated batches; (2) JEE/IPMAT crossover aspirants with heavy quant training; (3) fixed 40 DS seats even as applicant pool grows toward IIMB's stated scale-up. Drivers DOWN: possible seat expansion (IIMB plans 640 by 2031) and tougher papers depressing raw scores. Net model: +6 to +9 raw marks/year, tapering.

Reading The Composite Score

Final rank = 0.6 × (test score, normalized) + 0.4 × (PI score). At 40 seats, a 10-percentile PI swing ≈ several ranks. The winning profile: 130+ raw score AND a rehearsed, authentic interview. Neither alone is enough. Academic consistency in Class X/XII marks is reviewed at PI stage — protect your board scores; the 60%-in-Maths eligibility floor is a hard gate.
